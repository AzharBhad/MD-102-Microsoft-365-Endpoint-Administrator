## Community (unofficial)

Community blogs and videos are great for real-world context, but they may describe older portals or retired features (for example, Microsoft Endpoint Manager, Azure AD, Microsoft Store for Business). Always check against Microsoft Learn and the current study guide.

> This repository doesn't link to, use or endorse exam dumps or braindumps. They violate the Microsoft exam agreement and put your certification at risk.
